# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-05-27_

## What we collect

- **Account data:** email address (for sign-in) and Stripe customer ID (for paid users).
- **Local data:** transcripts, dictionary entries, snippets, and settings stay on your machine in a SQLite database. We never sync them to a server.
- **Audio:** Free tier transcription runs locally — audio never leaves your device. Pro tier streams audio to Deepgram for transcription; the audio is processed in-flight and not stored by us.

## How we use it

- Authenticate you and enforce your subscription tier.
- Process payments via Stripe.
- Operate cloud transcription and cleanup (Pro only).

## Third parties

We share data with these providers only as needed to deliver the service:

- **Supabase** — authentication, user profile, subscription state.
- **Stripe** — payments and billing.
- **Google OAuth** — sign-in (if you choose Google).
- **Deepgram** — cloud transcription (Pro only).
- **OpenAI** — text cleanup (Pro only).
- **ElevenLabs** — read-aloud (Pro only, optional).

## Your rights

- Export everything: Settings → Data and Privacy → Export.
- Delete everything: Settings → Account → Delete account.
- Email us to request hard-deletion from any third-party providers.
